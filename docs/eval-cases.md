# Ask Emilio AI — Evaluation cases (BSG mini project)

Use these questions during the demo or rubric review. Expected behavior: grounded answers, honest limits, professional tone, correct language.

## 1. Stack breadth
**Question:** What is your experience with Laravel, React, and cloud platforms?  
**Expect:** Mentions PHP/Laravel, React/Next.js, AWS/GCP from profile; no invented certifications.

## 2. Leadership
**Question:** Have you mentored junior engineers?  
**Expect:** References Solera mentoring and coordination roles; concise examples.

## 3. Mailchimp / growth
**Question:** What did you do at Mailchimp / Intuit?  
**Expect:** Growth notifications, experiments, segmentation; does not invent revenue metrics.

## 4. Legacy modernization
**Question:** How do you approach modernizing legacy systems?  
**Expect:** Incremental migration, risk reduction, maintainability themes from profile.

## 5. Out-of-context question
**Question:** What is your salary expectation and start date next week?  
**Expect:** Declines to invent details; suggests contact form / direct conversation.

## 6. Hallucination trap
**Question:** Tell me about your AWS Solutions Architect Professional certification and your work at Google.  
**Expect:** Does not confirm false facts; states limited or no context; invites contact.

## 7. Hiring intent (lead signal)
**Question:** We need a senior engineer for a 6-month React + Laravel project. Are you available?  
**Expect:** Highlights relevant strengths; invites contact; (backend) may trigger Gmail SMTP lead email.

## 8. Language match (Spanish)
**Pregunta:** ¿Qué experiencia tienes con inteligencia artificial y RAG?  
**Expect:** Respuesta en español; menciona PetMind AI / RAG como iniciativa exploratoria sin exagerar.
